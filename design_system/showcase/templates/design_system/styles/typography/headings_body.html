Headings and Body

The PLOS type scale, implemented in plos-overrides.min.css and matching the Figma "Responsive sizing" breakdown. Large screens apply from 40.0625em (641px); small screens are below 640px. Sizes are in px.

Type scale

To view the applied typography styles, inspect the elements in your browser's developer tools. Mobile screens will show the small screen sizes, and larger screens ( above 640px) will show the large screen sizes.
